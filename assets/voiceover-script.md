## ACT 1 — Hook (0:00–0:15, English, calm then tense)

[calmly] Eleven forty AM. Your mother's phone lights up.
Bank account frozen. Share the O T P now, or the police will arrest you today.
She panics. Her thumb hovers over the code.

## ACT 2 — The save (0:15–0:40, Hinglish, urgent)

[urgent] Ruko! O T P mat do! [pause] Unless Kavach aapke phone mein hai.
The SMS never buzzes. A calm siren rises instead. In ten seconds, you — at work —
read the full decrypted lure, tap Block, and when they call back, the phone kills
it before the first ring.

## ACT 3 — War-room proof (0:40–1:00, English, confident)

The Family War-Room shows everything: Safety Score one hundred, household fortified,
two parent devices sealed in a hardware keystore. Zero plaintext leaks to the cloud.
The manager opens the full decrypted text. The server sees only noise.

## ACT 4 — Pre-ring kill (1:00–1:15, Hinglish, triumphant)

[excited] Wahi number wapas call karta hai — aur phone pehli ring se pehle hi
call kaat deta hai! Then you both see the proof: the relay holds nothing but
ciphertext and hashes. Kill Switch revokes everything, in one tap.

## ACT 5 — It learns + pays (1:15–1:35, English, warm)

Unlike static blocklists, Kavach learns every morning — signed rules plus community
protection, without ever uploading a call. And it is a real business: Family Fortress
at seventy-nine ninety-nine a year, Pro Caregiver at four ninety-nine a month —
powered by RevenueCat, test mode for judges with promo Shipaton Judge. No card, no charge.

## ACT 6 — Close (1:35–1:50, Hinglish + English, heartfelt)

[warmly] Protection unke liye jo verify nahi kar sakte. Proof unke liye jo kar sakte hain.
Pause pressure. Verify independently. Bring family. [pause] Kavach — aapka kavach.
